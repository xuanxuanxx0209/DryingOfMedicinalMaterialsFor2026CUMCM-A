# 00 场景图生成说明

- 生成方式：Codex 内置图像生成工具（built-in image generation）
- 用途：数学建模论文候选概念图，尚未插入论文
- 输入参考图角色：用户提供的 3 张图片只用于理解“论文需要非数据型说明图”这一目的；未作为本图的编辑目标，也未复制其构图或具体内容

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: candidate illustration for a Chinese mathematical modeling paper about medicinal-material hot-air drying
Primary request: create a clean, realistic scientific cutaway illustration of a laboratory hot-air drying chamber containing one long cylindrical medicinal herb sample, showing the actual engineering scene clearly
Scene/backdrop: simple stainless-steel rectangular drying chamber in cross-section, inlet duct on the left and outlet duct on the right, uncluttered white-to-light-gray background
Subject: a single brown cylindrical medicinal herb sample, length much greater than radius, resting horizontally on a sparse perforated tray; warm air flows evenly around its curved side surface
Style/medium: polished 3D scientific illustration, realistic materials but schematic clarity, appropriate for an academic paper, not a glossy advertisement
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, chamber centered, sample large and readable, generous white margin, clear inlet-to-outlet flow; include only visual arrows, no labels
Lighting/mood: neutral soft studio lighting, precise and calm
Color palette: warm orange/red airflow arrows, cool blue moisture-vapor arrows emerging outward from the sample surface, muted brown sample, neutral gray chamber
Constraints: airflow arrows must travel from left inlet through chamber to right outlet; orange heat-transfer arrows point from surrounding hot air inward toward the cylindrical sample surface; blue moisture-transfer arrows point outward from the sample surface into the airflow; physically plausible and visually distinct; no people; no multiple herb pieces; no text; no numbers; no logos; no watermark
Avoid: decorative plants, herbs scattered around, kitchen oven appearance, food photography, dramatic smoke, fire, excessive vapor, inaccurate arrows, transparent labels
```

## 使用边界

该图是概念化场景，不代表题目给定或附件记录的真实烘房结构。若正式插入论文，图注应明确写“热风烘干过程概念示意”，不能写成“实验装置实物图”。
